BattTime puts your iPhone battery on your Apple Watch face. A look at your wrist is enough to see the level, whether it is charging and how old the value is.

CURRENT ENOUGH, NOT REAL TIME

BattTime updates regularly, about every 15 to 60 minutes depending on what watchOS and iOS allow, and right away when you open the app. Every value shows its age. After 60 minutes it is dimmed and marked as out of date, after 6 hours it is shown as unknown. Levels are never estimated.

ON YOUR WATCH

  • Complications for every family: circular, corner, rectangular and inline
  • Alerts when your iPhone drops below 20 % and 10 % (adjustable), once per threshold, never while charging
  • Find iPhone: plays a sound on your iPhone from your watch
  • History of both devices for 24 hours and 7 days

ON YOUR IPHONE

  • Widgets for the Home Screen and Lock Screen with your Apple Watch battery
  • Charging alerts: unplug at your target (80 % by default) and "watch fully charged"
  • Bedtime reminder to charge your watch when it is low
  • Live Activity while your iPhone charges, with an estimate based on your own measurements
  • Shortcuts and Siri: ask for the iPhone or watch battery and use the values in your own automations

PRIVACY

No account, no tracking, no ads, no analytics, no network. Your values stay on your iPhone and Apple Watch.

Requires iOS 18 and watchOS 11 or later and a paired iPhone and Apple Watch.
