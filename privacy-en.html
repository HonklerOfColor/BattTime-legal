On your iPhone and Apple Watch, the app is called “BatteryLeft+”.

Controller: Andreas Trabi, sole proprietor, Hönigtaler Straße 1/Top 12, 8010 Kainbach bei Graz, Austria, email: andreas.trabi@me.com. More details in the imprint.

Summary: BatteryLeft Companion only processes battery values of your iPhone and Apple Watch and your settings, exclusively on these two devices. No data is transmitted to me or to third parties. There is no account, no server, no network connection, no analytics, no tracking and no advertising.

What is stored locally (on iPhone and Apple Watch)

  • Battery values of your iPhone and Apple Watch: level, charging state, Low Power Mode and time of measurement.
  • A history of these values for the last 8 days (older values are deleted automatically).
  • Your settings (e.g. thresholds, charge target, reminder time) and the state of the alerts.
  • Diagnostic data about updates (when and through which channel a value arrived).

Exchange between iPhone and Apple Watch: This data is only transferred between your iPhone and your paired Apple Watch using Apple's WatchConnectivity. It never leaves these two devices and is not transmitted to me or to third parties. You delete it by deleting the app on both devices.

Notifications and Live Activity: Alerts (e.g. low battery, charge target, reminder, "Find iPhone") are local notifications created by the device itself. The app only asks for permission when you turn on an alert. The Live Activity is started and updated locally, without a push server.

Purposes and legal bases

  • Local app data: providing the features of the app (showing the battery, history, alerts). Legal basis: Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR.
  • Support requests by email or GitHub issue: handling your request. Your email address or GitHub user name and the content of your message are processed. Legal basis: Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR where the request concerns your purchase or use of the app, otherwise Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR (legitimate interest in answering requests). GitHub issues are public – please don't post private data there.

No sharing from the app: The app does not transmit any data to me or to third parties. It contains no third-party software for analytics, crash reporting or advertising.

No special data: BatteryLeft Companion does not process health data, location data or special categories of personal data under Art. 9 GDPR.

Your rights: You have the right of access, rectification, erasure, restriction of processing, data portability and objection (Art. 15–21 GDPR). As the app transmits no data to me, this usually only concerns support requests. You can lodge a complaint with the Austrian Data Protection Authority (www.dsb.gv.at).
