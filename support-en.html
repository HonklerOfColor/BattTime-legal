On your iPhone and Apple Watch, the app is called “BatteryLeft+”.

Why is the value not real time?

iOS and watchOS decide when apps may run in the background. The watch asks your iPhone regularly, usually every 15 to 60 minutes, and right away when you open BatteryLeft+ or tap Refresh. Every value shows its age; after 60 minutes it is dimmed, after 6 hours it is shown as unknown.

Adding the complication

Touch and hold the watch face, tap Edit, swipe to the complications, tap a slot and choose BatteryLeft+ → iPhone Battery. You can also edit watch faces in the Watch app on your iPhone.

“iPhone not reachable”

Your iPhone is out of range, switched off or in Airplane Mode. The app then shows the last value with its age.

I don't get alerts.

Alerts are off at first. Turn them on in Settings and allow notifications. Low battery alerts appear on the watch, never while the iPhone charges; they may be delayed by a few minutes depending on what iOS and watchOS allow. The charge target, “Watch fully charged” and the bedtime reminder appear on the iPhone. “Watch fully charged” needs a current level from the watch; while it sits locked on its charger the alert can come later or not at all.

I can't hear “Find iPhone”.

The app uses a time-sensitive notification with its own sound. In silent mode or with a Focus the sound may not be audible. Apple also offers its own feature in the Apple Watch Control Center to ping your iPhone.

Shortcuts

“Get iPhone Battery” and “Get Watch Battery” return the level as a number (−1 if unknown) and a sentence. Example: a personal automation at a time of day that runs “Get Watch Battery” and shows a notification for a value between 0 and 29. The app cannot switch Low Power Mode itself.

Live Activity

Starts when you open BatteryLeft+ while charging, with a button or with a shortcut. It is updated only while the app runs (when you open it, when your watch asks, or during a background refresh). It ends at your charge target; after you unplug, it ends the next time the app runs, not immediately. The remaining time uses measurements of the current charge, starting from the second one.
